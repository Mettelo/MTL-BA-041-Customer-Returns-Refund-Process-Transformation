# Mettelo Business Analyst Project Studio — MTL-BA-041

## Returns and refunds: process improvement and operational change

**Your role:** Process improvement Business Analyst. This is a fictional, synthetic BA simulation in professional UK practice.

**Your commission:** Which operational changes should Northbank fund to reduce refund delay and inconsistent handling within the revised £56,000 ceiling?

**Where your work ends:** An investment and operational change recommendation. Implementation and system UAT are outside this commission.

Start with [the project brief](project/PROJECT_BRIEF.md), then [the exact tasks, deliverables and acceptance criteria](project/TASKS_DELIVERABLES_AND_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md). Follow [delivery and evidence releases](project/DELIVERY_GUIDE.md), [timeline](project/PROJECT_TIMELINE.md) and [assessment](project/ASSESSMENT_CRITERIA.md).

| Task | What you do | What you submit | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Frame the improvement investigation | Problem statement, scope and evidence-backed baseline workbook | 20% |
| P2 | Diagnose current operations | AS-IS swimlane maps and root-cause analysis | 20% |
| P3 | Resolve policy and control gaps | Policy clarification and operational control proposal | 15% |
| P4 | Design improved operations | TO-BE process maps and operational procedure outline | 15% |
| P5 | Recommend an affordable intervention | Options appraisal and proportionate business case | 15% |
| P6 | Plan the operational transition | Change impact assessment, adoption plan and benefits measurement plan | 10% |
| P7 | Present the decision | Sponsor decision paper and final portfolio index | 5% |

## Evidence and boundaries
The [release manifest](project/RELEASE_MANIFEST.csv) distinguishes core sources, challenge notices and optional background. Historic source statements about an eight-week project, full requirements/UAT portfolio or operational pilot are stakeholder ambitions, not extra assignment requirements. Respond through this project's own outputs; a generic three-change report is not required. Later pilot observations are optional background and cannot be presented as tests you executed.
