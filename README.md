# Mettelo Business Analyst Project Studio — MTL-BA-041

## Returns and refunds: process improvement and operational change

**Role:** Process improvement Business Analyst · **Duration:** 6 cohort weeks · **Organisation:** Northbank Home & Style

This repository is the source library for a simulated business assignment. Your team acts as the BA team commissioned by Helen, the project sponsor. You investigate supplied evidence and create your own analysis in a separate delivery repository. All local organisations, people and operational records are fictional; clearly labelled official reference material remains external context.

## The business situation

Northbank Home & Style has commissioned this simulation. Returns move through stores, warehouses, customer service and Finance with inconsistent evidence, policy interpretation and hand-offs. Customers chase refunds while staff cannot always distinguish an authorised case, an accepted payment instruction and money received by the customer. Management sees delay but does not yet know which causes can be removed through working practices and controls.

### Example business situation

A customer starts a return online or takes goods to a store. A colleague captures the request; another team may receive and inspect the goods; an authorised person decides the refund; Finance passes an instruction to the payment provider. If a reference is missing, an instruction is rejected or the policy route is disputed, the case may return to an earlier queue. Customer Service sees a complaint but may not see a confirmed settlement. This is a business situation to investigate, not an approved process map.

### Why this work is needed

A process improvement decision must come before a system purchase. A new portal could simply make an unclear process more visible, while staff effort and unresolved payment exceptions remain. The business needs an evidence-backed proposal for how work and accountability should change.

### Perspectives you must reconcile

The sponsor wants faster refunds, Finance wants reliable reconciliation, stores need workable delegation, and warehouse colleagues need manageable inspection work. A faster step is not automatically a better end-to-end outcome if it increases errors or shifts work to another team.

## Your commission and the decision it supports

**Business question:** Which operational changes should Northbank fund, in what order and under whose ownership, to reduce avoidable delay and inconsistent decisions?

**Your endpoint:** A process improvement recommendation, proportionate business case and operational transition/benefits plan for leadership decision.

**What good work enables:** Leadership can see which delays and policy gaps are evidenced, compare credible interventions and decide on accountable operational changes. Proposed outcome measures must define their clocks, denominators, owners and limitations; no achieved saving or improvement is supplied.

**Skills you will practise:** Process investigation, AS-IS/TO-BE modelling, root-cause reasoning, operational controls, options appraisal and change adoption.

## What your team will actually do

1. **P1 — Frame the improvement investigation.** Produce problem statement, scope and evidence-backed baseline workbook. Use it to inform the next task and keep unresolved decisions visible.
2. **P2 — Diagnose current operations.** Produce aS-IS swimlane maps and root-cause analysis. Use it to inform the next task and keep unresolved decisions visible.
3. **P3 — Resolve policy and control gaps.** Produce policy clarification and operational control proposal. Use it to inform the next task and keep unresolved decisions visible.
4. **P4 — Design improved operations.** Produce tO-BE process maps and operational procedure outline. Use it to inform the next task and keep unresolved decisions visible.
5. **P5 — Recommend an affordable intervention.** Produce options appraisal and proportionate business case. Use it to inform the next task and keep unresolved decisions visible.
6. **P6 — Plan the operational transition.** Produce change impact assessment, adoption plan and benefits measurement plan. Use it to inform the next task and keep unresolved decisions visible.
7. **P7 — Present the decision.** Produce sponsor decision paper and final portfolio index. Use it to inform the next task and keep unresolved decisions visible.

Each task has four explicit assignment acceptance criteria in [TASKS_DELIVERABLES_AND_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md](project/TASKS_DELIVERABLES_AND_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md). They explain how the output is assessed. Where you must write solution acceptance criteria, those are your own proposed business/system/product behaviours, not supplied answers.

## Scope and practical boundaries

**Included:** Store and online return-to-refund work, policy routing, authorisation, inspection, payment exceptions, reconciliation and customer status communication. Proposed systems changes may be dependencies, but the core work is operating-process improvement.

**Outside this assignment:** No replacement-system FRD, detailed interface specification, agile product backlog, executable system or full system UAT portfolio is required. Participants do not issue legal determinations or process real refunds.

**Planning constraints:** The initial one-off planning envelope is £80,000, including £8,000 committed; the annual incremental ceiling is £15,000. Later released funding evidence may constrain the recommendation. Include internal effort, exclusions and recurring costs; a quote is not a complete business case.

6 weeks describes your learning commission; any dated operational pilot in source testimony is a business ambition, not an instruction to deploy. Later pilot/experiment files are optional background. Do not claim you conducted the supplied interviews, executed tests or achieved live benefits. No paid tool is required; use readable documents, spreadsheets and diagram/prototype tools you can access.

## Why there are change scenarios

The notices introduce new facts, contested proposals or constraints that test your judgement. Apply them to this project's outputs and keep a revision trail. They do not make every project a change-management assignment and do not require a separate generic three-change report.
