# Project timeline

| Week | Evidence release | Participant activity and review |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | R1 | Initiation, scope, hypotheses and elicitation plan; sponsor playback |
| 2 | R2 | Interviews, follow-ups and stakeholder validation |
| 3–4 | R3 | Current state, data quality, rules and requirements |
| 5 | R3 | Future state, options, prioritisation and traceability; baseline review |
| 6 | R4 notices 1, 2, 3 in order | Impact assessment after each notice; re-baseline with decisions |
| 7 | R5 pilot | UAT planning, observations, defects and readiness assessment |
| 8 | R5 follow-up | Change, benefits evaluation and final playback |

Suggested effort: 40–60 hours across eight weeks. Evidence dates are simulated business dates; week numbers are relative to cohort start. Never infer that the pilot is your chosen design or a production deployment.
