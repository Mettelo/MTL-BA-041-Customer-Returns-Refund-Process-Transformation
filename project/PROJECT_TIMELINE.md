# MTL-BA-041 — 6-week pathway

Weeks are relative to cohort start. Original source dates remain part of the fictional history; the facilitator may compress that history into release weeks.

| Week | Work and checkpoint |
|---|---|
| 1 | Frame the commission and inspect initial evidence. Review relevant task criteria and retain a dated revision. |
| 2 | Analyse the core evidence and conflicting accounts. Review relevant task criteria and retain a dated revision. |
| 3 | Develop the central pathway outputs. Review relevant task criteria and retain a dated revision. |
| 4 | Apply challenge evidence and revise the analysis. Review relevant task criteria and retain a dated revision. |
| 5 | Validate coherence and unresolved decisions. Review relevant task criteria and retain a dated revision. |
| 6 | Handover the decision and final baseline. Review relevant task criteria and retain a dated revision. |

Begin tasks 1–2 in weeks 1–2; develop tasks 3–5 in weeks 3–4; validate task 6 in week 5 and finalise task 7 in week 6.

The [manifest](RELEASE_MANIFEST.csv) controls when sources become available. Optional later experiment material has no mandatory UAT task. No elapsed simulation week constitutes stakeholder approval or an executed implementation.
