## Timing of later evidence

The delivery pack is compiled with follow-up totals through 23 August and is therefore issued in full on **24 August**, after the Week 7 experiment and the participant's acceptance-preparation snapshot. In Week 7, the facilitator may role-play the scheduled sessions without revealing the results. The compiled full pack is not available before the final follow-up week closes.

Stage 6 is the lifecycle stage of the evidence, not a promise that it is all available at the start of Week 6. The manifest assigns the full delivery pack and pilot CSV to Stage 6; the release log must also record the 24 August availability date.

## Later operational milestones are proposals

The desired operational pilot is **12 October 2026**, after this analysis engagement. The configuration freeze begins **1 November**. The OrderHub write-back slot later confirmed for **16 November** is after both. The August experiment is an isolated synthetic replay, not evidence that an operational October pilot has already succeeded.

The final participant recommendation must distinguish an analysis decision, an experiment, an implementation commitment and a production readiness decision. No calendar milestone overrides policy, control or evidence needs.

## Release discipline

Save initiation, discovery/current-state, pre-change baseline, each change response and final assurance/recommendation versions in your own delivery repository. Use meaningful commits and pull requests rather than one end-of-project upload. A facilitator should release each notice only after the preceding impact response is recorded.

If a review cannot validate an assumption, record its status and consequence. Missing approval is not supplied by the passage of a week. Participants may recommend escalation, conditional progress or deferral with reasons. Final submission follows the separate-repository model in [submission guidelines](../submission/SUBMISSION_GUIDELINES.md).
