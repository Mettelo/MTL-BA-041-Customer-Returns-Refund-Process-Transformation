# Assessment criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence expected |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem and scope | 10 | Challenges assumed solution; measurable need and clear boundaries |
| Elicitation and collaboration | 15 | Purposeful questions, conflicting perspectives and validation trail |
| Current state and evidence | 15 | Own models; reproducible measures; quality limitations and plausible causes |
| Requirements and rules | 20 | Clear testable needs, exceptions, non-functional/transition needs and source traceability |
| Options and priorities | 15 | Genuine alternatives including minimal change; costs, risks and justified trade-offs |
| Change response | 10 | Versioned impacts and decisions for all three notices |
| Assurance and value | 15 | Independent UAT, defect decisions, readiness, adoption and benefits ownership |

Score each criterion 0–4: 0 absent; 1 asserted with little evidence; 2 partly supported with material gaps; 3 coherent and supported; 4 critical, traceable and validated with uncertainty handled. Weighted total = sum(weight × score / 4). Suggested pass: 60/100, with no zero in requirements or assurance. This is a learning rubric, not professional certification.

Do not reward agreement with a presumed solution. Return work for revision if it presents invented stakeholder approvals, real personal data, or a simulation as actual client employment. Assess reasoning, accessibility, control implications and consistency, not document volume.
