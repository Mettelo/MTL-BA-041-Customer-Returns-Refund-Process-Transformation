# MTL-BA-041 — delivery guide

## 1. Understand the commission
Read [PROJECT_BRIEF.md](PROJECT_BRIEF.md) and [the task/acceptance specification](TASKS_DELIVERABLES_AND_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md). Your endpoint is: An investment and operational change recommendation. Implementation and system UAT are outside this commission.

## 2. Build an evidence trail
Create your own source register, questions and assumptions as working aids. Preserve CSVs unchanged and record cohort, units, joins, missing data and cutoff assumptions for calculations. Reference source paths and record IDs. Supplied stakeholder accounts are fictional elicitation inputs, not research conducted by you. A blank field does not prove an event never happened.

## 3. Use staged releases
Release 1 contains the commission, organisation/sponsor context and interviews. Release 2 adds operational data, policy, system evidence and the pathway-specific source supplement. Release 3 adds the three challenge notices. They are inputs to this pathway's decisions, not three mandatory change-management deliverables. Release 4 is optional later experiment evidence, available only after your own analysis is baselined. The facilitator should check [RELEASE_MANIFEST.csv](RELEASE_MANIFEST.csv) before distribution.

For the four-week discovery, use releases in weeks 1, 2 and 3; finish in week 4. For other pathways, use releases in weeks 1, 2 and 4, then finish on the project timeline. Retain the version before challenge evidence and explain material revisions within relevant outputs.

## 4. Produce the distinct outputs
- **P1:** Problem statement, scope and evidence-backed baseline workbook.
- **P2:** AS-IS swimlane maps and root-cause analysis.
- **P3:** Policy clarification and operational control proposal.
- **P4:** TO-BE process maps and operational procedure outline.
- **P5:** Options appraisal and proportionate business case.
- **P6:** Change impact assessment, adoption plan and benefits measurement plan.
- **P7:** Sponsor decision paper and final portfolio index.

Use proportionate detail and consistent IDs. Document unanswerable questions and the evidence/owner needed. Do not fill gaps with invented facts. These source materials intentionally do not contain a completed FRD, process map, decision table, interface mapping, backlog or other learner answer.

## 5. Review and submit
Review every task acceptance criterion. Cross-check terms, diagram boundaries, numerical claims, proposed behaviours and unresolved decisions. Follow [submission guidance](../submission/SUBMISSION_GUIDELINES.md) for a separate repository, accessible history and tagged final index. A review log may record self-review where no peer is available; do not fabricate a reviewer.


## Team handover check

Use [the project-specific deliverables checklist](../submission/DELIVERABLES_CHECKLIST.md) to verify every required component and final submission record. The checklist specifies packaging; the task acceptance criteria define analytical quality.
