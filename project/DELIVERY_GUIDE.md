# Delivery guide

## Run the engagement

Use PROJECT_TIMELINE.md and RELEASE_MANIFEST.csv. Before opening a new release, save a dated decision snapshot and questions. Each source has an author/perspective or a stated extraction basis; it need not be complete or correct. Cite file paths and record IDs. Do not treat interview statements as automatically approved requirements.

R1: establish the problem, scope, hypotheses, initial measures and who else to engage. Gate: sponsor playback and outstanding questions.

R2: conduct simulated elicitation using the interviews. Prepare follow-up questions, reconcile conflicting objectives and document unresolved decisions. Gate: evidence of stakeholder validation attempts.

R3: analyse cases, exceptions, rules and system constraints; construct your own models, requirements and options. Gate: reasoned recommendation with traceability and uncertainties.

R4: release the three change notices sequentially, after the learner has saved a baseline. Reassess impacts, priorities, costs, risks and decisions after each notice. Gate: versioned impact assessment and sponsor decision request.

R5: examine supplier pilot observations independently. Define tests from your own requirements before reviewing outcomes. Assess defects, release readiness, training, cutover/rollback and benefits. Gate: recommendation and executive playback.

## Facilitation

Distribute only manifest paths for released stages; do not give learners a full clone for a gated cohort. The public source library cannot prevent spoilers. Record date, cohort, release, pack version and learner acknowledgement outside this repository. Ask participants to request missing evidence explicitly. A facilitator may answer only from supplied evidence; otherwise record 'not established' or issue a clearly labelled additional synthetic source to the entire cohort. Do not invent approvals or guide learners to one solution.

No facilitator model answers are included. For individual work, document questions that would require a real stakeholder rather than inventing interview responses.

## Tooling

A text editor, spreadsheet tool and diagramming tool are sufficient. No paid licence or external account is required. CSV is UTF-8, dates ISO 8601, GBP values exclude VAT unless noted. Preserve original sources and put participant analysis in submission/ or a separate portfolio repository.
