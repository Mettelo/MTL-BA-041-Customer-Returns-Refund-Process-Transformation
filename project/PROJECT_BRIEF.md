# MTL-BA-041 — commissioned assignment

**Mettelo Business Analyst Project Studio · distinct pathway edition**

## Business problem and reason for commissioning the team

Returns move through stores, warehouses, customer service and Finance with inconsistent evidence, policy interpretation and hand-offs. Customers chase refunds while staff cannot always distinguish an authorised case, an accepted payment instruction and money received by the customer. Management sees delay but does not yet know which causes can be removed through working practices and controls.

A process improvement decision must come before a system purchase. A new portal could simply make an unclear process more visible, while staff effort and unresolved payment exceptions remain. The business needs an evidence-backed proposal for how work and accountability should change.

Read the [organisation and sponsor context](../business_context/BUSINESS_CONTEXT_AND_SPONSOR_BRIEF.md) for the observed operating environment.

## Role and business decision
You are the Process improvement Business Analyst. Which operational changes should Northbank fund to reduce refund delay and inconsistent handling within the revised £56,000 ceiling?

An investment and operational change recommendation. Implementation and system UAT are outside this commission.

## Business situation and intended value

A customer starts a return online or takes goods to a store. A colleague captures the request; another team may receive and inspect the goods; an authorised person decides the refund; Finance passes an instruction to the payment provider. If a reference is missing, an instruction is rejected or the policy route is disputed, the case may return to an earlier queue. Customer Service sees a complaint but may not see a confirmed settlement. This is a business situation to investigate, not an approved process map.

Leadership can see which delays and policy gaps are evidenced, compare credible interventions and decide on accountable operational changes. Proposed outcome measures must define their clocks, denominators, owners and limitations; no achieved saving or improvement is supplied.

## Scope

Store and online return-to-refund work, policy routing, authorisation, inspection, payment exceptions, reconciliation and customer status communication. Proposed systems changes may be dependencies, but the core work is operating-process improvement.

No replacement-system FRD, detailed interface specification, agile product backlog, executable system or full system UAT portfolio is required. Participants do not issue legal determinations or process real refunds.

## Working agreement
Use supplied facts, conflicting stakeholder accounts and synthetic source records. Make calculations reproducible; distinguish evidence, interpretation, proposal and unresolved approval. Reference actual source paths and row/case IDs. Do not invent stakeholder sign-off, real research, executed tests or realised benefits. External references supply context, not automatic approval of your proposals.
