**Release 2 · fictional source material, not an approved solution**

## Operations manager follow-up, 27 July 2026
The payment team says its daily queue starts with authorised cases, while Customer Service counts days from the first customer contact. Store staff record collection at a till; the warehouse records an inspection later. Finance will not accept a target that hides cases waiting for authorisation. The sponsor asks the analyst to establish which clocks answer which business question. No single clock or target is approved.

## Practical constraints
A store can spare one colleague for a 45-minute training session per week. The warehouse can alter inspection batching without a new contract, but cannot promise extra headcount. Finance requires a named owner for reconciliation and an explicit action when an instruction is rejected. Customer Service needs a message that explains status without promising funds have reached a customer. These are stakeholder concerns, not a designed future process.
