# Existing forms and customer communications

**Mettelo Business Analyst Project Studio · MTL-BA-041 · Stage 3**  
**Source copies:** in use at 30 June 2026 · All synthetic  
These are existing operational source documents. Blank fields and omissions are intentional evidence, not a future design template or completed BA deliverable.

## 1. Branch acceptance pad — revision December 2025

**Northbank Home & Style — goods accepted for review**

Branch: ______  Date: ______  Colleague initials: ______  
Web order / till receipt reference: ______  
Item description and quantity: ______  
Customer's stated reason: ______  
Customer contact preference: ______  
Goods received: yes / no  
Manager initial: ______

Printed footer: “Thank you. Your refund is normally completed within 5 days. Keep this acknowledgement.”

The pad does not distinguish physical custody from an eligibility decision, central authorisation, provider submission or settlement. It has no mandatory central return ID, version of policy, timestamp of transfer, inspection detail or named recipient of a hand-off. Customers receive the original. Local retention and attachment to the central case vary.

The form has one ambiguous goods/evidence field. No controlled field records when Northbank first took custody at a branch. A query does not identify its next owner or expiry. The source does not prove that a photograph is always retained, that a manager reviews every match, or that a workbook entry is immutable.

## 4. Existing web help copy — published 12 May

“Changed your mind? Return eligible unused items within 28 days. For online purchases you can use post or bring the goods into a store. We aim to complete your refund within five days of receipt. Some exclusions apply. Contact us if you need help.”

This is the text supplied to the BA, not approved wording for future use. It does not distinguish the commercial offer from all statutory rights or define the relevant receipt event. The content editor's review note says “shorten for mobile”, with no attached cross-functional sign-off.

## 5. Service email macro — local library, last edited 3 April

“Thank you for your return. Once our warehouse confirms your return, please allow up to five working days for Finance to process your refund. We will contact you if we need anything else.”

Aisha cannot confirm that every adviser uses this version. The macro says working days; the web copy says days. It does not distinguish provider submission from customer receipt or describe what happens when the provider rejects the instruction. Do not adopt it without policy and communication review.

## 6. Adviser status message — exported example

Subject: “Your refund has been sent”  
Body: “Your case has been marked refunded. If your money is not visible, please contact your bank.”

The retained configuration allows an adviser-selected label to trigger this message. No provider-reference condition was demonstrated at discovery. The source does not establish how often the message was sent in the 1,400-case population. R0852 is a reason to investigate timing and visibility; the pack does not claim this exact message was sent for that case.

## 7. Counter instruction note — uncontrolled printout

“Online returns: take goods and receipt, email Service, send to depot. Ask a manager over £150. For a customer without a receipt ask Service before agreeing.”

The note has no author, issue date or approval record. It may be incomplete, superseded or misapplied. Its existence does not make it an authoritative eligibility rule. Jo reports that Finance's separate £100 email is kept behind it in S07.

## 8. Purpose for the participant

Use the forms and messages to investigate information capture, ownership, customer understanding, controls and exceptions. Determine what evidence would be needed to validate current practice and what future needs arise. The pack intentionally does not provide redesigned forms, field specifications, acceptance criteria or an approved communications plan.
