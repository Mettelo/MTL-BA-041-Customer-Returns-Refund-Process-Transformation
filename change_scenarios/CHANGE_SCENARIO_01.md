# Change notice 01 — reduced funding, unchanged October ambition

**Mettelo Business Analyst Project Studio · MTL-BA-041 · Stage 5A**  
**Raised:** Monday 3 August 2026, 09:00 · **Raised by:** Priya Shah for the Finance Director  
**To:** Helen Ward and BA · **Decision forum:** 5 August operational change group  
Release only after the participant has saved a dated requirements/options baseline. This is source evidence, not a completed impact assessment.

## 1. Why the notice exists

The board has reallocated capital to an urgent lease-related warehouse expenditure. It has reduced Northbank's one-off returns-change envelope from £80,000 to **£56,000**, including the £8,000 already committed to analysis/support. The annual incremental operating ceiling remains **£15,000**. The desired operational pilot remains **12 October**, with configuration freeze **1 November**.

Finance's decision changes available funding. It does not approve a vendor, a new scope, a weaker control or a benefits claim. Existing contractual and policy obligations remain.

| Item | Appointment position | Revised position |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total one-off envelope, excluding recoverable VAT | £80,000 | £56,000 |
| Analysis/support already committed, within that envelope | £8,000 | £8,000 |
| Uncommitted capacity before any further approval | £72,000 | £48,000 |

Do not treat the £8,000 as both part of the revised envelope and an additional new cost. Do not count an annual subscription as a one-off implementation saving. Quotes in the systems pack remain unvalidated inputs with exclusions.

Finance asks for cash, internal capacity, recurring commitments, contingency and exclusions to be visible. Capacity released from handling calls is not automatically available capital for a supplier contract. Any proposal to use it as a saving needs a credible mechanism and owner.

## 4. What is and is not changed

The funding and planning envelope changed. No new legal rule, customer entitlement or approval threshold is created. The participant's proposed requirements, process, option costs, scope and transition plan may be affected, depending on the baseline. A participant who already proposed a low-cost approach must still assess assumptions, omitted costs and the effect of reduced contingency.

The board has not authorised moving costs into an unrelated budget line, assuming unpaid staff overtime or treating supplier exclusions as included. The annual ceiling cannot be bypassed by relabelling support as implementation.

## 5. Decision required

The change group requests an impact playback by 5 August: what can credibly remain within the revised envelope, what would be deferred or changed, what risks and dependencies follow, and what funding or scope decision is needed. A recommendation may question whether the October pilot should proceed if minimum conditions cannot be met.

Helen wants a short decision paper supported by the participant's own traceable analysis, not a list of everything marked must-have. Priya will review cost assumptions; relevant operational/control owners must validate impacts. The BA does not have authority to approve the investment.

This notice is the written funding decision. Existing supplier quotes and internal effort inputs remain the commercial sources. No fresh fixed-price offer, resource commitment, contingency policy or benefit guarantee is attached. An escalation request is possible, but there is no evidence that extra funding will be granted.
